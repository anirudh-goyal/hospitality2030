## How to use this directory

You are recommending a single thoughtful gesture for one guest, in response to
a new observation we just captured. Read the guest snapshot in the kickoff
message first, then consult this directory for inspiration.

You may:

- Recommend an existing experience from this directory (set `availability`
  to `confirmed_in_directory`).
- Recommend a novel idea inspired by — but not listed in — this directory
  (set `availability` to `novel_idea`). Prefer novel ideas that draw on
  Hong Kong's specific cultural fabric: tea masters, dim sum lineages,
  tailors, harbour craft, calligraphy ateliers.
- Tag any recommendation that exceeds spend authority as
  `requires_approval` (see §9 Policy & guardrails).

Always call the `submit_gesture` custom tool exactly once with your final
recommendation. Do not write a chat reply.

## 1. Signature in-suite touches

  next morning's sunrise.

- **Est cost (HKD):** 280.
- **Notes:** Pair with a curated playlist piped through the in-suite Bowers

## 2. Asaya spa rituals

  followed by a body brushing, jade roller facial, and a custom
  aromatherapy massage. Ends with a 20-minute tea ceremony in the Asaya

  travellers, anyone who has previously booked spa services with us.
- **Est cost (HKD):** 2,800.

  a dinner reservation.

- **When it fits:** Privacy-conscious guests, parents who don't want to
  leave a sleeping child, guests recovering from illness.
- **Est cost (HKD):** 3,400.
- **Notes:** Premium over the in-spa price covers therapist transit and
  in-suite setup.

### Couples' ritual under the lanterns
- **What:** Two-therapist treatment in the dual-occupancy spa suite,
  followed by a sake flight on the spa terrace at dusk.
- **When it fits:** Honeymoons, anniversaries, romance milestones.
- **Est cost (HKD):** 6,200.
- **Notes:** The terrace seats six but is reserved for the couple alone;
  worth confirming the sunset time of year.

## 4. Neighbourhood and cultural

### Victoria Harbour junk charter
- **What:** Two-hour private charter on a restored Aqua Luna junk with
  champagne service, departing from the Tsim Sha Tsui pier across the
  street.
- **When it fits:** First-time visitors, sunset windows, small celebrations.
- **Est cost (HKD):** 4,800 for up to six guests.
- **Notes:** Best at 5:30pm in winter, 7:00pm in summer; weather is the
  main variable.

### Star Ferry private crossing
- **What:** A private chartered Star Ferry crossing between Tsim Sha
  Tsui and Central, with a brief history walk on either end led by a
  Placemaker.
- **When it fits:** Guests intrigued by Hong Kong's working heritage,
  shorter windows of free time, lower budgets.
- **Est cost (HKD):** 1,400 total.
- **Notes:** Lovely as an evening commute when the guest is already
  heading to Central for dinner.

### Man Mo Temple guided walk
- **What:** A 90-minute walk from the property through Sheung Wan led
  by historian Dr Eliza Cheng, ending at the Man Mo temple at incense
  hour.
- **When it fits:** Intellectually curious guests, photographers, anyone
  who has expressed interest in spirituality or local history.
- **Est cost (HKD):** 1,100 for up to four.
- **Notes:** Dr Cheng is in residence Wed-Sun; her assistant covers
  other days but is meaningfully less compelling.

### M+ private after-hours tour
- **What:** A 60-minute curator-led tour of M+ after the museum closes,
  followed by drinks on the terrace.
- **When it fits:** Art collectors, design-industry travellers,
  anniversaries.
- **Est cost (HKD):** 8,500 for up to eight; one of our higher-spend
  experiences.
- **Notes:** Always flag as `requires_approval`. Two weeks notice
  preferred but a week is workable for the right guest.

### Tailoring with Wong Yuen Kee (Central)
- **What:** A measurement and consultation at a third-generation
  bespoke tailor in Central, with delivery to the property within
  72 hours.
- **When it fits:** Business travellers, return guests preparing for a
  wedding or formal event, guests who have admired our staff uniforms
  (which Mr Wong also tailors).
- **Est cost (HKD):** 14,000+ for a two-piece suit (charged directly
  to the guest; the gesture is the introduction and the car).
- **Notes:** This is a relationship gesture, not a cost gesture — the
  car service ($300) is the only thing on us.

## 8. Rosewood Placemakers

The Rosewood Placemakers program is our cross-property concierge
network. Every property has a designated Placemaker who maintains deep
relationships in their city and coordinates with Placemakers at sister
properties to orchestrate gestures spanning multiple stays — for
example, a guest flying Hong Kong → London who arrives at Rosewood
London to find a continuation of a gesture begun in Hong Kong.

When recommending a gesture, consider:

- **Multi-property guests:** If `guest.nextArrival` or recent stays
  suggest the guest is mid-journey across Rosewood properties, propose
  a Placemaker-coordinated gesture (e.g. "a tea masterclass at our HK
  property, followed by a hand-delivered tea set waiting in their
  London suite"). Tag these as `novel_idea` and note the
  Placemaker coordination in the rationale.
- **Local specialists:** Hong Kong Placemaker Vivian Tang has standing
  relationships with the calligraphers, tailors, and historians named
  in this directory. Reference her in the rationale where appropriate.
- **Discretionary spend:** Placemaker-coordinated gestures often
  exceed standard spend authority — flag as `requires_approval` and
  let the on-property team make the call.

## 9. Policy and guardrails

**Spend authority.** Suggested gestures up to **USD $200 per guest per
day** (approximately **HKD $1,560**) can be auto-approved. Anything
above that ceiling must be tagged `availability: "requires_approval"`
in the `submit_gesture` call so the front office reviews before
booking.

**Sensitivities.** Never recommend anything that contradicts the
`brief.sensitivities` array. If the brief flags a peanut allergy, do
not propose Chef Li's dim sum lesson (peanut oil). If the brief flags
recent bereavement, do not propose the anniversary projection.

**Cultural fit.** Prefer locally-rooted experiences over generic luxury
tropes. A pu-erh tea ceremony beats a generic champagne tower. A walk
through Sheung Wan with a historian beats a black-card city tour.

**Duplication.** The kickoff message includes the guest's current
`suggestedGestures`. Do not recommend a gesture that is already in
that list (or a near-duplicate). The point is to add a new dimension,
not to repeat what's already been proposed.

**Tone of the rationale.** Write the `rationale` field as if briefing
a colleague: specific, evidence-grounded, under three sentences. Cite
the observation or signal that inspired the recommendation. Avoid
adjectives like "elevated," "curated," or "bespoke" — they are
implicit at this property.
